KnightDesk bundles CPython 3.12.10 (see Python.txt), psutil 7.2.2
(see psutil.txt), and dynamically loaded Qt/PySide6/Shiboken 6.8.3.

Qt, PySide6 and Shiboken are provided under the GNU LGPL version 3.
The accompanying GPL and LGPL texts apply to those components.
The components are installed as replaceable DLLs and Python modules.
You may replace them with interface-compatible builds and may reverse
engineer KnightDesk for debugging your modifications to those components.
